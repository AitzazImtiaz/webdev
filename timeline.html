    What shape shouts ?!

    From the play animation, and collecting the data of news, three things stick out to me strikingly.

    First, as noted above, infrastructure thread loads earlier on and by the time fibre upgrades happen and 5G arrives, there is no significant fire in thread. My inference developed here is that by the time 5G arrived in 2019, physical network development is essentially finished in Korea. Future work nay happen in specialized labs and development might grow, but the coverage of physical auxilliary cables seems connected.

    Second, policy is 'reactive.' The Shutdown Law with peering dispute, following consequential behaviour, the law is widespread; regulations almost follow the usage of network than shaping it mostly. Essentially, my inference here is that since most technology is developed, Korea has been pushing ethics and focusing on refining safety of users, as compared with leading countries around. e.g. Singapore.

    Third, contrary to what you are seeing, the platform thread is even but it never stops! KakaoTalk, Netflix launching there landscape in K-dramas, webtoon boom, and especially Squid Game layered into a widespread boom to Korean culture. It would be wrong to ignore dramas in Korean ethno-society, these have shaped as a revolutionary to heal Korea's past poverty and dictatorship era. My inference is supported by a detailed documentary on origin's of K-drama with how they lifted off most censorship. The documentary may have political content.

    About data

    Source
    The collection of information is stored in JSON which you may access from opening and expanding the table, it records selected events, their dates, threads, and notes.
    Validation Criterion
    Dates must be strictly of format YYYY-MM, where ids are unique primary keys. Tampering with the JSON data will not refer partial timeline but render failures on top.
    Rendering
    Using the latest P5.js technology, canvas was rendered as distinct entity from the same array which loads the table, so they cannout get out-of-sync.
