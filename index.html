Twenty Years – Connection Spark

The following site lists history of South Korea beyond 2006. Although a country which I will probably not visit, my inspiration of choosing Korea has been both from its enriched history, including inspiration of Samsung, in what Asians can achieve, to the prowess it commanded in K-dramas. By my birth, fibre cable was in almost many apartment blocks, PC Bangs were full, and question stopped from being, how to go online and became what do we attain from it now that everyone uses it. This site will follow and attempt to answer the second question across those twenty years since.

My main goal in this site is to represent it as a case study rather than an overt survey where each page takes a thread –– wires, gaming café, platforms, rules etc. and follow the timeline from 2006, all down to present, with events and interactive figure sources. Since I am treating this site like a case study, or more like a "case book," I will make claims rest on numbers, and the numbers cited inline, listing again at the foot page to make references accessible.

Historically, the headline figure in Korea sits highly accessible. By January of 2024, 97.2% of population in Korea was online and more mobile connections existed than people. Median fixed connection in Korea following high connectivity, reached 233 Mbps, up nearly by around 50% in a single year. As a psychological inference, fast and ubiquitious speeds to load network pages, the argument for internet being fast just turned out to be no longer an exceptional merit!
