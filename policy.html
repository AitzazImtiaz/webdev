Five years of laws

2007 onwards, any site above certain traffic threshold was required to verify the legal identity of its maker with an aim to curb abuse. As an effect, a national register was formed for surveillance to maintain ethics.

By 23 August 2012, the Constitutional Court struck that rule down, finding in the public interest that justifying this restriction was too minute, observing that users had simply move to foreign platforms where the rule cannot be reached. A law designed to monitor public speech had been relocated.

As a counteragument, it can be noted that the breach on policy database's influenced other tables. In July 2011, there were about 35 million Nate and Cyworld accounts getting exposed in a country consisting of 49 million people. This means mandatory identity verification means certain records exist, and stealing them breaches privacy.

Decade of midnight

The Shutdown Law was placed into effect in 20 November 2011, blocking every teenager below 16 from online game servers between mignight and 6 am. Hence Korea became the first country to try national gaming curfew, while surviving constitutional hurdles in 2014.

However, this law did not survive on phones as mobile games were never covered as scope and by the time two ministries announced to repeal in August 2021, mobiles already accounted for majority of market which the law aimed to govern. This curfew ended on 31 December 2021, getting replaced by a system where guardians set the limits under parental controls.

Yet... a restriction outlived. Under-19s are till date barred from PC bangs and gaming venues between 22 pm and 9 am under Game Industry Act's enforcement decree. The law regulating room outlasted the law regulating games.

How long things lasted

"How long each law lasted" preset computes duration from enacted and ended columns; rows with no end date are measured as running till date, which is why the premises restrictions keep growing.

The calculation happens in SQLite within your browser and not in page's JavaScript.
